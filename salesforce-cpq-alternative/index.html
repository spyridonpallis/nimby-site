Questions

Before you book.

Do we need a different Salesforce edition?

Standard Quotes come with Sales Cloud. Some approval and automation features depend on your edition, so we check yours on the review call before we quote.

What happens to our old CPQ quotes?

We export them before anything is uninstalled. The history stays on each opportunity as data, as PDFs, or both. Your choice.

How long does it take?

Most CPQ sunsets take a few weeks end to end. We plan from your notice date so the old license can't renew.

What does it cost?

About half of one year's CPQ license, as a fixed price agreed before you commit, with a $2,500 minimum. Half at signing, half at go-live.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything lives in your org and is documented, with no package of ours to license.
