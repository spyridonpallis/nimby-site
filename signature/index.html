Add it in Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear at the top right, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Nimby".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Cmd+V on a Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

If you send from more than one address, Gmail lets you pick a default signature for each one under Signature defaults.
