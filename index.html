Approach

How a sunset works.

Four steps, and most sunsets take a few weeks end to end. We work back from your notice date so the old tool is gone before it can renew.

The renewal date isn't your deadline. The notice date is.

  1. 01

    Inventory

    Every installed package, connected app, and integration user, with its cost, owner, renewal date, and notice date.

    You getAn inventory sheet you keep using after we're done.

  2. 02

    Decide

    A keep, replace, or renegotiate call on each tool. Replacements come with a fixed quote and the month they pay for themselves.

    You getA written recommendation and a fixed quote.

  3. 03

    Rebuild

    Built in a sandbox first. Data moves with a reconciliation report, so every record is accounted for.

    You getA working build your team signs off on.

  4. 04

    Retire

    Cutover, a clean uninstall, and the non-renewal notice sent before the deadline. We archive the old data before access ends.

    You getA closed contract and a smaller bill.
